Incident Report IR-2025-004

Water Main Break, Alder Street between 4th and 6th Avenues
Date:Monday, 3 February 2025
Prepared by:Tomás Ibarra, Operations Superintendent
Report date:10 February 2025
Asset:8-inch cast-iron main, installed 1968 (asset ID DM-0412)

1. Summary

A circumferential break on the 8-inch cast-iron main under Alder Street caused a loss of pressure for 214 customers. Service was restored after about eleven hours. A boil-water advisory was issued for the affected area and lifted on 6 February 2025 after two consecutive days of negative bacteriological samples. No illness was reported.

4. Cause and recommendations

The main failed from external corrosion, made worse by frost loading. It is the third break on cast-iron mains from the 1960s in two years. The Operations Department recommends:

  1. an accelerated program to replace the remaining cast-iron mains (about 6.8 miles), starting in FY2026;
  2. adding two isolation valves on Alder Street so a future break affects fewer customers; and
  3. a second after-hours crew member during January and February.

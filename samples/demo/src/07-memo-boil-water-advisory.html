Subject:Updated procedure for boil-water advisories

This memo replaces Memo No. 2021-05. It takes effect immediately and applies to every part of the distribution system.

When to issue an advisory

  • Pressure falls below 20 psi anywhere in the distribution system;
  • a main break or repair causes a loss of pressure; or
  • a routine sample is positive for E. coli.

Steps

  1. Notify the General Manager and the County Health Department within one hour.
  2. Issue the advisory to the affected customers within two hours: SMS alert, website banner and door hangers. Customer service posts the map of the affected area.
  3. After service is restored, collect samples at three locations in the affected area on two consecutive days.
  4. Lift the advisory only when every sample from both days is negative and the General Manager has approved. Notify customers through the same channels.
  5. Record every step, with times, in the incident log and attach it to the incident report.

Questions about this procedure go to me or to the on-call supervisor.

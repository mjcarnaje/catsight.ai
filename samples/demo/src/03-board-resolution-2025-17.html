Board Resolution No. 2025-17

Board of Directors · Regular Meeting of 9 April 2025

SUBJECT: Award of the Contract for the Rehabilitation of Pump Station 4 to Halverson & Pike Mechanical, Inc.

WHEREAS, Pump Station 4 on Quarry Lane, built in 1987, supplies the eastern pressure zone and its two pumps have exceeded their service life, as reported in the 2024 Asset Condition Assessment;

WHEREAS, the Cooperative invited bids for the rehabilitation of Pump Station 4 on 3 February 2025 and received four bids by the closing date of 7 March 2025;

WHEREAS, Halverson & Pike Mechanical, Inc. submitted the lowest responsive bid of One Million Two Hundred Eighty-Four Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($1,284,500.00), and the General Manager recommends the award;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Directors of the Tamsin Ridge Water Cooperative:

  1. That the contract for the rehabilitation of Pump Station 4 is awarded to Halverson & Pike Mechanical, Inc. for $1,284,500.00;
  2. That the cost is charged to the Capital Reserve Fund, with a contingency of ten percent (10%) that the General Manager may use without further Board approval;
  3. That the General Manager, Dana Okafor, is authorized to sign the contract on behalf of the Cooperative.
Moved by:Director Hana Sato
Seconded by:Director Ruth Abernathy
Vote:Five (5) in favor; one (1) opposed (Director Karol Ostrowski); one (1) absent

ADOPTED this 9th day of April 2025.

Miguel Ferreira
Miguel Ferreira, Board Chair
Priya Raman
Attest: Priya Raman, Board Secretary

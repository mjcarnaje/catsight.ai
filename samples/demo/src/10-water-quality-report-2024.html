2024 Annual Water Quality Report

Consumer Confidence Report for water delivered from 1 January to 31 December 2024 · Published 30 June 2025

Where your water comes from

In 2024, 78 percent of your water came from the Tamsin Ridge Reservoir and was treated at the Cedar Bend Treatment Plant (coagulation, filtration and chlorine disinfection). The other 22 percent came from Wells 2 and 3, which draw from the Upper Marl aquifer and are disinfected at the wellhead.

Lead in drinking water

Lead in drinking water comes mainly from materials in service lines and home plumbing. The Cooperative has no lead service lines in its inventory (updated October 2024). If water has been sitting for several hours, run the tap for 30 seconds before drinking or cooking.

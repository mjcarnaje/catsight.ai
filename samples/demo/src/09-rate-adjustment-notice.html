Notice to Customers: Water Rate Adjustment

Effective for water used from 1 July 2025 · Issued 1 June 2025

Dear Customer,

At a public hearing on 14 May 2025, the Board of Directors adopted Resolution No. 2025-22, adjusting water rates for the first time since 2021. The new rates apply to water used from 1 July 2025 and appear on bills mailed from August.

Why rates are changing

A cost-of-service study by Clearwater Rate Advisors found that current rates do not cover the Cooperative's capital program, which includes the rehabilitation of Pump Station 4 and the replacement of about 6.8 miles of cast-iron water mains installed in the 1960s, the same kind of main that broke on Alder Street in February.

What it means for a typical household

A household using 6,000 gallons a month pays $48.10 instead of $44.20, an increase of $3.90 per month.

Help paying your bill

Households enrolled in a state energy or food assistance program can receive a credit of $10.00 per month through the Customer Assistance Program. Apply at the Cooperative's office or call customer service.

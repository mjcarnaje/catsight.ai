Travel and Per Diem Policy

Policy No. 2025-03 · Approved by Board Resolution No. 2025-09 · Effective 1 April 2025

1. Purpose

This policy sets out how employees and directors of the Tamsin Ridge Water Cooperative travel on official business and how their travel costs are approved, advanced and reimbursed. It replaces Policy No. 2019-11.

2. Approval of travel

  1. Every trip outside Tamsin Ridge County needs a Travel Request approved by the employee's supervisor and the Finance Officer, Lena Kowalczyk, at least ten (10) working days before departure.
  2. Travel by the General Manager is approved by the Board Chair. Travel by directors is approved by the Board.
  3. Trips abroad also need the written approval of the General Manager.

3. Per diem rates

The per diem covers meals and incidental expenses. It is paid for each calendar day of travel; the day of departure and the day of return are paid at 75 percent.

DestinationPer diem per dayLodging (actual, up to)
Within the state$65.00$160.00 per night
Outside the state$90.00$210.00 per night
Outside the country$120.00$260.00 per night

No per diem is paid for day trips of less than eight (8) hours, but meals bought on such trips are reimbursed at actual cost up to $25.00 per day.

4. Transportation

  • Cooperative vehicles are used whenever one is available.
  • Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.62 per mile, measured from the employee's usual workplace.
  • Air travel is booked in economy class through the Finance Office.

5. Reimbursement

A Travel Expense Report with original receipts for lodging, transportation and registration fees must be submitted to the Finance Office within fifteen (15) days after the employee returns. Reports submitted later than sixty (60) days are not reimbursed. Unused cash advances are returned with the report.

6. Exceptions

The General Manager may approve exceptions in writing when the published rates are clearly insufficient, for example during a major conference. Exceptions are reported to the Board each quarter.

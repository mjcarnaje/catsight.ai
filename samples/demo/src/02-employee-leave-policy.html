Employee Leave Policy

Policy No. 2023-07 · Effective 1 January 2024 · Owner: Human Resources (Grace Whitlow)

1. Who this policy covers

All regular full-time employees of the Tamsin Ridge Water Cooperative. Part-time employees earn leave in proportion to their scheduled hours. Temporary and seasonal staff are covered by their employment agreements.

2. Vacation leave

  • Employees earn 1.25 days of vacation leave per month of service (15 days per year). After ten years of service the rate rises to 1.5 days per month.
  • Vacation is requested at least five working days in advance through the leave system and approved by the supervisor.
  • Up to ten (10) unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Days above that are forfeited on 31 December unless the General Manager approved a carry-over in writing.

3. Sick leave

  • Employees receive ten (10) days of sick leave each calendar year. Unused sick leave accumulates up to sixty (60) days.
  • A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days.
  • Sick leave may also be used to care for a sick spouse, child or parent.

4. Parental leave

An employee who becomes a parent by birth or adoption may take up to twelve (12) weeks of parental leave within the first year, paid at 70 percent of regular salary. The leave may be taken in up to two blocks.

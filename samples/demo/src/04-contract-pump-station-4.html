Construction Agreement

Contract No. TRWC-2025-041 · Rehabilitation of Pump Station 4

This Agreement is made on 22 April 2025 between the Tamsin Ridge Water Cooperative (the "Owner"), 100 Reservoir Road, Tamsin Ridge, and Halverson & Pike Mechanical, Inc. (the "Contractor"), 4410 Foundry Street, Brackenfield, under Board Resolution No. 2025-17.

Article 1. Scope of work

The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials and equipment to rehabilitate Pump Station 4, Quarry Lane, including:

  1. removal of the two existing 125-horsepower pumps and installation of two new 150-horsepower vertical turbine pumps;
  2. installation of variable frequency drives and a new motor control center;
  3. integration of the station into the Owner's SCADA system;
  4. replacement of the station's discharge header and isolation valves; and
  5. testing, start-up and training of the Owner's operators (at least eight hours).

Article 2. Contract price

The Owner shall pay the Contractor $1,284,500.00, subject to additions and deductions by approved change orders. Progress payments are made monthly against approved applications for payment, less a retainage of five percent (5%), which is released at final acceptance.

Article 4. Warranty

The Contractor warrants all work and equipment for two (2) years from final acceptance and shall correct any defect within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

Article 5. Insurance and bonds

The Contractor shall maintain general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and provide performance and payment bonds, each for 100 percent of the contract price.

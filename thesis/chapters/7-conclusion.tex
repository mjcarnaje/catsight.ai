\chapter{Conclusion and Recommendations}\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Conclusion}

This study set out to develop and evaluate a content-based document retrieval system for MSU-IIT that integrates OCR, vector embeddings, and LLM-powered conversational interaction. Each specific objective (Section~\ref{sec:objectives}) was addressed as follows.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Collection.} A corpus of 50 issuances was assembled from IIT Docs and the BOR Resolutions archive across six categories, including scanned, typewritten, watermarked, and table-heavy files (Table~\ref{tab:corpus}). Two exact duplicates were detected automatically, leaving 48 documents.
  \item \textbf{Design.} The architecture (Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}) combines a resumable document pipeline, hybrid retrieval that fuses semantic and keyword search, and a single-agent chat that answers from retrieved passages with page-level citations. Every model is open-weight, so the institution can host the whole system on its own hardware (Table~\ref{tab:gpu}).
  \item \textbf{Implementation.} The pipeline, search, and chat were implemented and deployed as a public demonstration. Marker 2 with Surya OCR 2 reads scanned pages, with Qwen3-VL correcting tables, forms, and handwriting; documents are catalogued zero-shot, with map-reduce for long texts; and the agent's answers stream with citations that open the source page.
  \item \textbf{Evaluation.} Among the converters compared, Marker with its LLM mode and the vision-model approaches recovered by far the most facts from scanned pages, while MarkItDown recovered none (Section~\ref{sec:converter-comparison}). In the evaluation of the defended prototype, users rated \cs{} at 77.17 on the System Usability Scale against 50.33 for the existing system (Section~\ref{sec:usability-results}). \TODO{Add the retrieval-accuracy and summary-quality results of Sections~\ref{sec:retrieval-results} and~\ref{sec:summary-results} once measured.}
  \item \textbf{Analysis.} The results and the issues met during development (Chapter~\ref{ch:design}) lead to the recommendations below.
\end{enumerate}

Overall, \cs{} shows that the limitations of keyword search in MSU-IIT's repositories---semantic blindness, the exclusion of scanned files, and rigid interaction---can be addressed with open models: users ask in their own words, scanned issuances become searchable, and every answer can be checked against the page it came from.

\section{Recommendations}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Complete the evaluation of the rebuilt system.} Repeat the usability study, and run the retrieval and summary evaluations of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation-procedure} with a larger set of questions and human-written reference summaries, including questions in Filipino and Cebuano.
  \item \textbf{Host the models on campus.} Serve the open models on an institutional GPU server (Table~\ref{tab:gpu}) so that no document text leaves the campus network, as the Data Privacy Act may require for confidential records; the local configuration of Section~\ref{sec:local-mode} already supports this.
  \item \textbf{Scale the library.} Ingest the complete IIT Docs and BOR archives. Beyond a few hundred thousand passages, add an approximate vector index (Section~\ref{sec:issue-ann}) and measure its effect on filtered searches.
  \item \textbf{Read images and seals.} Describe photographs, diagrams, and seals with the vision model, and recognize signatures, which the current system records only as ``[signature]''.
  \item \textbf{Index tables on their own.} Reading a short document whole fixes questions within one document (Section~\ref{sec:issue-table}); for questions across the whole library, index each table as a passage of its own together with the sentence that introduces it, and keep stamps that OCR turns into headings out of the section names.
  \item \textbf{Learn from use.} Let users mark answers as helpful or not, and use the feedback to tune the similarity threshold, the reranker gate, and the instructions.
  \item \textbf{Integrate with the repositories.} Index new issuances automatically as they are posted to IIT Docs, instead of uploading them by hand.
  \item \textbf{Serve other institutions.} Apart from the cataloguing instructions and the tag list, nothing in the pipeline is specific to MSU-IIT. Let several organizations share one installation, each with its own library, members, tags, and model provider and key, so that each bears its own usage costs and can choose local models (Section~\ref{sec:local-mode}); every search and chat must then be restricted to the organization's own documents (Section~\ref{sec:data-model}). Other offices and universities with scanned records could then use \cs{} without a deployment of their own.
\end{enumerate}
